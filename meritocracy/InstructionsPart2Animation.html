Examples

If the Performance rule applies with a 20% chance, this can be interpreted as follows:

{{ if player.framing == 'iq' }}
  • In 20 out of 100 cases, the winner is the participant who solved more puzzles in Part 1.
  • In the remaining 80 out of 100 cases, the computer selects the winner at random, giving both participants an equal chance.
{{ else }}
  • In 20 out of 100 cases, the winner is the participant who solved more questions in Part 1.
  • In the remaining 80 out of 100 cases, the computer selects the winner at random, giving both participants an equal chance.
{{ endif }}

If the Performance rule applies instead with an 80% chance, this can be interpreted as follows:

{{ if player.framing == 'iq' }}
  • In 80 out of 100 cases, the winner is the participant who solved more puzzles in Part 1.
  • In the remaining 20 out of 100 cases, the computer selects the winner at random.
{{ else }}
  • In 80 out of 100 cases, the winner is the participant who solved more questions in Part 1.
  • In the remaining 20 out of 100 cases, the computer selects the winner at random.
{{ endif }}
